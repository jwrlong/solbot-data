# Profitable wallets (2026-09-29/30, 23.2 hours of Pump.fun + PumpSwap)

Every wallet with at least 5 positions it opened **and fully closed** inside the data window and at least 1 SOL realized profit **after venue fees and Jito tips**. 4012 wallets qualify; the full list with all columns is in [`profitable-wallets.csv`](profitable-wallets.csv). Generated by `scripts/wallet_report.py`.

**Read with care:**
- **One day of data.** A wallet that was profitable on one day may not be the next.
- **Positions still open at the end** (or opened before the data started) aren't counted.
- **Ordinary priority fees aren't recorded**, so real profits are slightly lower.
- **Style labels are inferred from behaviour** (the rules are in the script).
- **Most of these wallets can't be profitably copied.** See [FINDINGS.md](../FINDINGS.md) §13: their edge is being first, being part of the launch, or reacting in the same slot. Copying 5 lists of these wallets lost money in every test, even on the data they were picked from.

**CSV columns:** net/gross profit and fees in SOL; positions = closed positions; `median_entry_slots_after_launch` (bonding-curve buys, 1 slot = 0.4 s); `launch_slot_buy_pct` = curve buys in the launch block; `graduation_buy_pct` = graduated tokens first bought within 2 slots of graduation; `backrun_pct` = curve buys landing right after a sell in the same slot; `deployer_tokens` = tokens the wallet itself launched; `drain_cluster_link` = tied on-chain to the launder-and-drain clusters.
